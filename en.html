Frequently asked questions

Your questions, my answers

How much does a repair cost?

I give you a price over the phone as soon as you have described the problem, travel included. If the situation on site is different, I tell you before starting.

Do you come in the evening and at weekends?

You can reach me Monday to Saturday. For a major leak outside these hours, leave a message: I call back as soon as possible and explain how to turn off the water in the meantime.

What should I do while waiting for you?

Turn off the main water supply, usually near the meter, and the electricity if water is touching a socket or an appliance. Take a photo, it helps me come with the right part.

Is your work guaranteed?

Yes. My jobs are covered by a 10-year building warranty and professional liability insurance. The certificates are sent on request.

Do you service gas boilers?

Yes. The annual service includes cleaning, adjustments and safety checks. You receive the certificate your insurer asks for.

Do you do complete bathrooms?

Yes. I take care of the plumbing and coordinate the tiler and electrician I usually work with. You keep one single contact.
